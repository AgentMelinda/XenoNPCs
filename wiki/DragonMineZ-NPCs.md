# DragonMineZ NPCs

A Xeno NPC is a DragonMineZ fighter: it has DMZ stats, can transform, glows with an aura, knows DMZ
skills and fires ki attacks - all set from the **DMZ** tab of the [editor](The-Editor).

## Stats and race

DMZ strength, ki power, defence and the rest are set per NPC. Its **power level** - the number ki
sense and the scouter show - comes from those stats the same way a player's does.

## Skills

The **Skills** page lists every skill DragonMineZ knows. A **new NPC starts with every skill switched
on at its maximum level**; switch off what it should not have. The Fly skill here is what lets the
NPC fly in a fight.

## Techniques and attacks

**Techniques** and **Attacks** pick the ki blasts, ki waves and strikes it uses, and the animations its
punches play.

## Forms

**Forms** lists the DragonMineZ transformations it can use. With the combat brain on, an NPC can
transform during a fight.

## Appearance

DMZ looks: hair, colours, the aura, and the rest of what makes it look like a DMZ character.
