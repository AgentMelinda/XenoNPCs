# CustomNPCs and My NPCs

XenoNPCs works on its own. If you also have **CustomNPCs** or **My NPCs** installed (only one of
them - they are the same mod under two names), XenoNPCs brings its DragonMineZ features to those NPCs
as well:

- extra **DMZ** screens in their NPC editor (stats, skills, forms, aura, appearance, attacks, brain);
- the **combat brain** for their NPCs;
- DragonMineZ calls in their **scripts** (race, stats, forms, ki attacks).

Their own features keep working exactly as before.

## Moving CustomNPCs / My NPCs into Xeno NPCs

`/xenonpcimport` brings existing CustomNPCs or My NPCs NPCs over as Xeno NPCs. See [Commands](Commands)
for its options.
