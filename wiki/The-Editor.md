# The Editor

Right-click an NPC with the **Xeno NPC Wand** to open it. The tabs run along the top, in this order:

**Display · Stats · AI · Inventory · Advanced · Global · DMZ · Brain · Delete · X**

A small live preview of the NPC shows your changes before you save.

## Display

Name, model and looks: what the NPC is called and how it appears in the world.

## Stats

Health and how it takes and deals damage, with its own sub-pages:

- **Respawn** - whether, where and how fast it comes back.
- **Melee Properties** - its punches.
- **Ranged Properties** - distance attacks (including the *Accuracy* its ki blasts lead with).
- **Projectile Properties** - what it throws.
- **Resistance** - how much of each kind of damage it shrugs off.

## AI

How it behaves when it is not fighting, including **Movement** (standing, wandering, following a
route you drew with the Path Tool).

## Inventory

What it holds and wears, and what it drops.

## Advanced

Everything that gives an NPC a job or a personality. Depending on its role you see pages for
**Trader**, **Transporter**, **Bank**, **Guard**, **Bard**, **Healer**, **Follower** and **Item Giver**,
plus:

- **Lines** - things it says in speech bubbles.
- **Factions** - who it likes and who it attacks.
- **Dialogs** - conversations players can have with it.
- **Sounds**, **Night**, **Linked**, **Scenes**, **Marks**.

## Global

Things shared by every NPC in the world, not just this one:

**Banks · Factions · Dialogs · Quests · Transport · PlayerData · Recipes (no bench) · Natural Spawns · Linked**

## DMZ

The DragonMineZ side of the NPC - see [DragonMineZ NPCs](DragonMineZ-NPCs):

**Skills · Techniques · Forms · Appearance · Attacks**

## Brain

Turns the **Combat brain** on and sets what it may do - see [Combat Brain](Combat-Brain).

## Delete

Removes the NPC. Press **Delete** twice - the first press only asks you to confirm.

## X

Closes the editor.
