# Roles, Dialogue and Quests

## Roles

Every NPC has a role. You choose it when you place the NPC (sneak + right-click with the wand cycles
through them) and can change its details in the editor's **Advanced** tab.

| Role | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Humanoid** | A plain person - the starting point for anything. |
| **Creature** | A creature-shaped NPC: shorter and wider than a person. |
| **Trader** | Sells and buys items (Advanced > Trader). |
| **Guard** | Stands guard over an area (Advanced > Guard). |
| **Companion** | Follows a player around (Advanced > Follower). |
| **Quest** | Gives quests. |
| **Transporter** | Takes players to other transport points (Advanced > Transporter, Global > Transport). |
| **Bank** | Stores items for players, in tabs, and pays out in Zeni (Advanced > Bank, Global > Banks). |

## Speech bubbles and lines

**Advanced > Lines** sets things the NPC says on its own - they pop up in a speech bubble above its
head that everyone nearby sees.

## Dialogue

Conversations are made in **Global > Dialogs** (shared, so many NPCs can use the same one) and given
to an NPC in **Advanced > Dialogs**. A dialogue is a set of lines and answer options; options can lead
to other dialogues, start quests and more.

## Factions

**Global > Factions** creates factions; **Advanced > Factions** says which one an NPC belongs to and
how it treats the others.

## Quests

Quests are made in **Global > Quests** and handed out through dialogue - step by step in [Making Quests](Making-Quests). When a player
finishes one, a completion popup shows the reward.
