# Your First NPC

Everything below needs **operator permission** (level 2) - on your own world, turn cheats on.

## 1. Get the wand

Open the creative inventory, find the **XenoNPCs** tab, and take the **Xeno NPC Wand**.

## 2. Choose what kind of NPC

**Sneak + right-click** (in the air or on a block) cycles the NPC type:

Humanoid, Creature, Trader, Guard, Companion, Quest, Transporter, Bank.

Pick **Humanoid** for your first one. The type only decides what the NPC starts as - every NPC can be
changed in the editor afterwards.

## 3. Place it

**Right-click a block.** The NPC appears standing on it, facing you.

## 4. Open the editor

**Right-click the NPC** with the wand. The editor opens on the **Display** tab - give it a name and
change how it looks. Everything you change is saved when you press save. See [The Editor](The-Editor).

## 5. Find it again later

**Right-click the air** with the wand: the **Nearby NPCs** screen lists every Xeno NPC around you, and
you can select one from there.

## The other tools

All in the XenoNPCs creative tab. Each one works on the NPC you click with it.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Path Tool** | Draws an NPC's patrol route: bind it to an NPC, then click blocks to lay out where it walks. |
| **Cloner** | Copies a whole NPC - looks, stats, dialogue, trades, route - so you can place copies. Great for eight identical guards. |
| **Jar** | Picks an NPC up and puts it down somewhere else. The NPC leaves the world while it is in the jar. |
| **Mounter** | Right-click the NPC, then right-click what it should ride (a horse, a boat). Use in the air to ride the NPC yourself. Sneak + right-click the NPC to get it off. |
| **Teleporter** | Right-click the NPC to hold it, right-click a block to send it there. Sneak + use in the air to pick a dimension. |
| **Script Tool** | Opens the script screen for the NPC you use it on. See [Scripting Basics](Scripting-Basics). |

## Zeni

The creative tab also holds **Zeni** coins and notes (1 up to 1,000,000). Bank NPCs pay in them - see
[Roles, Dialogue and Quests](Roles-Dialogue-Quests).
